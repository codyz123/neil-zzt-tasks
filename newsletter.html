New to Pickleball?

We’ll get you
on the court.

Zero Zero Two isn’t just for experienced players. Our free beginner clinics (101–104) take you from “what’s the kitchen?” to confident rallying in four sessions. Small groups, patient instructors, all equipment provided.

101 — THE BASICS

Rules, grip, serve, rally

102 — THE KITCHEN

Dinking, volleys, positioning

103 — SHOT SELECTION

Drops, drives, placement

104 — GAME PLAY

Strategy, stacking, doubles

Free for members. $25 drop-in for non-members. Bring a friend — they’ll thank you later.
